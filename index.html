Our Story

The Living Oracles of God began as an inspired vision: to make high-quality, practical education accessible to Christians worldwide. We recognized that standard tuition architectures often prevent aspiring global leaders from expanding their knowledge base.

By blending core ministerial theology, business mechanics, and emerging digital systems, we built a comprehensive online ecosystem. Heavily supported by the ADUSEI Foundation, our key focus centers on empowering African youth, dismantling socio-economic bottlenecks, and fostering modern leadership skills through open-access education.

Statement of Faith

Our structural curriculum answers completely to the following foundational beliefs:

  • The Trinity: We believe in one true God, eternally co-existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.
  • The Scriptures: The Bible is the fully inspired, infallible, and absolute authoritative Word of God for daily living.
  • Salvation: True redemption is received purely through grace by faith in Jesus Christ alone, confirmed via His death, burial, and bodily resurrection.
  • Marketplace Mandate: Enterprise and tech development are strategic callings intended to bring order to societies and glorify God.

Fees & Financial Aid

The Pay-As-You-Gift Model

Our courses remain entirely tuition-free. We use a faith-driven, collaborative support layout called the Pay-As-You-Gift model, allowing students to voluntarily support the platform backend if they have the means.

Suggested Support Contribution: $10 - $50 per block. Accepted pathways include secure Credit Cards, PayPal, and local Mobile Money (MoMo).
