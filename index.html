Who We Are

Living Oracles Global Institute (LOGI) is a faith-based institution offering tuition-free education. Our courses are 100% online, collaborative, and practical.

We believe that world-class vocational, professional, and ministerial education should be accessible to everyone, regardless of location or financial status.
