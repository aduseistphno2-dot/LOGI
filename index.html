About Our Story

Bridging the gap between timeless faith and cutting-edge global innovation.

🎯 The Genesis

The Living Oracles Global Institute was established to answer a critical global challenge: the artificial separation of academic excellence, technological expertise, and deep spiritual baseline training. Founded in Accra, Ghana, we set out to build an accessible, borderless workspace where students do not have to choose between their spiritual callings and professional marketplace mastery.

🚀 The Evolution

Recognizing that legacy university tuition fees continuously lock out millions of gifted leaders across developing economies, we threw away the traditional commercial structure. By migrating entirely onto the open-source Moodle infrastructure and coordinating with key international ministries and philanthropic drivers, we deployed an advanced, enterprise-grade, tuition-free digital workspace canvas.

🌍 The Horizon

Today, our interactive campus serves an expansive, multinational student body spanning several countries. Through our pay-as-you-gift ecosystem and targeted skill paths across engineering, modern enterprise, and biblical theology, we are actively structuring an elite generation of ethical marketplace professionals positioned to transform global systems.

👁️ Our Vision Statement

To operate as the premier global center for contextual learning, establishing a unified network of kingdom-minded industry experts, technologists, and ministers who execute excellence across every cultural mountain.

⚡ Our Core Mandate

To dismantle financial barriers to elite training by providing fully responsive, localized, and practical skills development programs embedded directly inside foundational biblical truths.

📜 Welcome Message

Welcome to The Living Oracles Global Institute! This handbook is designed to help you navigate your learning journey successfully. Please read it carefully and refer back to it whenever you have questions about policies, procedures, or expectations.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide accessible, practical, and faith-integrated education that equips students for impactful service in ministry, business, and engineering fields worldwide.

🔑 1. Registration and Account Setup

1.1 Creating Your Account

Complete the online application form on our website. You will receive an email with your Moodle login credentials within 48 hours.

1.2 Account Security

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your password. Do not share your login credentials with anyone. If you suspect unauthorized access, change your password immediately and contact support.

1.3 Personal Information

Keep your profile information up to date, including email address and phone number, as we use these for important communications.

🖥️ 2. Course Enrollment and Access

2.1 Enrolling in Courses

Once logged into Moodle, you will see available courses. Click "Enroll" to add a course to your dashboard. You can enroll in multiple courses simultaneously.

2.2 Course Structure

Each course is organized into modules or weeks. Modules typically include video lessons, reading materials, quizzes, and practical assignments.

2.3 Access Duration

You have indefinite access to courses you have enrolled in. There are no expiration dates. You can review materials even after completing the course.

⏱️ 3. Self-Paced Learning Model

3.1 No Deadlines

Our courses are completely self-paced. There are no strict deadlines for completing modules or assignments. You set your own schedule.

3.2 Recommended Pacing

While there are no deadlines, we recommend dedicating 3-5 hours per week to each course to maintain momentum and retain information effectively.

3.3 Progress Tracking

Your Moodle dashboard shows your progress through each course, including completed modules, quiz scores, and assignment submissions.

📊 4. Assessments and Grading

4.1 Quiz and Exam Policy

Most courses include quizzes to reinforce learning. You may retake quizzes as many times as needed. Your highest score will be recorded.

4.2 Assignments

Some courses require practical assignments, such as sermon outlines, business plans, or technical projects. Submit these through the Moodle assignment portal.

4.3 Passing Requirements

To receive a certificate, you must complete all modules and achieve an average score of 70% or higher on quizzes and assignments.

📌 Grading Scale:
90 - 100%: Excellent | 80 - 89%: Very Good | 70 - 79%: Satisfactory | Below 70%: Insufficient (retake required)

⚖️ 5. Academic Integrity

5.1 Original Work Policy

All assignments and quiz responses must be your own original work. Plagiarism, copying, or unauthorized collaboration is strictly prohibited.

5.2 Artificial Intelligence Use

AI tools may be used as learning aids but not to complete assignments on your behalf. If you use AI for research or drafting, you must disclose this explicitly inside your submission.

5.3 Consequences of Violations

First violation: Warning and opportunity to resubmit. Second violation: Automatic failure of the assignment. Third violation: Account suspension and revocation of all issued certificates.

🛠️ 6. Communication and Support

6.1 Technical Support Desk

For login issues, platform errors, or technical problems, email support@livingoraclesofgod.com. Standard response time is within 48 hours.

6.2 Academic Support

For questions about course content, assignments, or grades, email academics@livingoraclesofgod.com.

6.3 WhatsApp Priority Channel

Reach us on WhatsApp at +233 24 145 6581 for urgent inquiries. Operating Hours: Monday - Friday, 9am - 5pm GMT.

6.4 Overall Response Window

We strive to respond to all inquiries within 2 business days. During high-volume intake periods, response times may vary slightly.

💬 7. Community Guidelines

7.1 Telegram Global Community

All enrolled students are invited to join our Telegram group for peer support, discussions, and prayer requests. The invitation link is sent via email automatically post-registration.

7.2 Respectful Communication

Treat all community members with respect. Harassment, hate speech, bullying, or discriminatory language will result in immediate removal from the group and possible account suspension.

7.3 Appropriate Content Filter

Do not share inappropriate, offensive, or explicit content in any institute-affiliated channel. This includes unsolicited spam, advertisements, or external promotional links.

7.4 Privacy in Groups

Respect the privacy of fellow students. Do not share personal details, contact lists, or screenshots from private workspace chats without clear permission.

🎓 8. Certificate Issuance Framework

8.1 Digital Certificates

Upon successfully completing a course, you can download your digital certificate directly from your Moodle dashboard. Digital certificates are always completely free.

8.2 Printed Certificates

If you require a physical printed certificate, you may request one for a nominal fee to cover global printing production and shipping logistics. Contact certificates@livingoraclesofgod.com for custom processing.

8.3 Secure Verification Dashboard

Each certificate includes a unique verification ID. Employers, churches, or organizations can securely verify authenticity through our global Verification system portal.

8.4 Lost Replacements

If you lose your files, you can re-download the digital version from Moodle at any time. For replacement premium physical configurations, reach out to our administration office.

🔌 9. Technical Requirements

9.1 Internet Connection Baseline

A stable internet connection with at least 2 Mbps download speed is recommended. Our system infrastructure is perfectly optimized for 3G/4G networks.

9.2 Device Cross-Compatibility

Our platform is accessible across smartphones, tablets, laptops, and desktop computers. Modern standard browsers such as Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge are recommended.

9.3 Moodle Mobile Native Ecosystem

Download the official Moodle mobile app from Google Play Store or Apple App Store for offline cached access to courses and multi-device cross-progression.

9.4 Required Software Applications

Most courses require no additional external software. Specific technical setups or free programming configurations will be noted inside the respective course modules syllabus.

🎁 10. Scholarship and Financial Aid

10.1 ADUSEI Foundation Scholarship

The ADUSEI Foundation provides full tuition scholarships to students from underserved communities, particularly across Africa. All students automatically qualify; no separate application steps are needed.

10.2 Hardware and Data Support

In unique exceptions, the ADUSEI Foundation may provide internet data bundles or mobile device assistance to individuals facing extreme context challenges. Contact scholarships@livingoraclesofgod.com to query your case.

10.3 Voluntary Giving

Students who are capable may choose to make voluntary donations to support the platform's cloud operations and help seed open-source scholarships for others. Contributions are optional and non-refundable.

📝 11. Grievance and Appeals

11.1 Grade Discrepancies Appeals

If you believe an assessment query was graded incorrectly, you can submit a tracking query within 14 days of grading cycles. Email academics@livingoraclesofgod.com tracking the course details.

11.2 Violation Policy Appeals

If you believe an academic integrity flag or suspension restriction was placed incorrectly, submit an appeal review to appeals@livingoraclesofgod.com detailing context evidence.

11.3 Committee Process Review

Appeals are systematically evaluated by an independent academic board committee within 14 business days. Determination actions are final.

⏳ 12. Withdrawal and Inactivity

12.1 Voluntary Course Withdrawal

If you wish to withdraw from a course, simply stop accessing it. There is no complex formal withdrawal process. You may resume at any time.

12.2 Extended Dormant Status

Your account profile remains active indefinitely. There is no penalty for extended periods of inactivity. You can resume your studies whenever you are ready.

🤝 13. Code of Conduct Summary

As a student of The Living Oracles Global Institute, you explicitly agree to:

  • Complete your own work with the highest levels of honesty and integrity.
  • Treat fellow students, staff, and instructors with extreme respect.
  • Use platform server resources responsibly, legally, and ethically.
  • Respect the intellectual property privileges of the institute.
  • Represent the institution positively in all professional external settings.

🔒 14. Legal Acknowledgment

By enrolling in our courses, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to abide by the policies outlined in this Student Handbook, as well as our global Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy documentation.
