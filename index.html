Our Story

The Living Oracles Global Institute began as an inspired vision: to make high-quality, practical education accessible to Christians worldwide. We recognized that standard tuition architectures often prevent aspiring global leaders from expanding their knowledge base.

By blending core ministerial theology, business mechanics, and emerging digital systems, we built a comprehensive online ecosystem. Heavily supported by the ADUSEI Foundation, our key focus centers on empowering African youth, dismantling socio-economic bottlenecks, and fostering modern leadership skills through open-access education.

📜 Welcome Message

Welcome to The Living Oracles Global Institute! This handbook is designed to help you navigate your learning journey successfully. Please read it carefully and refer back to it whenever you have questions about policies, procedures, or expectations.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide accessible, practical, and faith-integrated education that equips students for impactful service in ministry, business, and engineering fields worldwide.

1. Registration and Account Setup

1.1 Creating Your Account
Complete the online application form on our website. You will receive an email with your Moodle login credentials within 48 hours.

1.2 Account Security
You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your password. Do not share your login credentials with anyone. If you suspect unauthorized access, change your password immediately and contact support.

1.3 Personal Information
Keep your profile information up to date, including email address and phone number, as we use these for important communications.

2. Course Enrollment and Access

2.1 Enrolling in Courses
Once logged into Moodle, you will see available courses. Click "Enroll" to add a course to your dashboard. You can enroll in multiple courses simultaneously.

2.2 Course Structure
Each course is organized into modules or weeks. Modules typically include video lessons, reading materials, quizzes, and practical assignments.

2.3 Access Duration
You have indefinite access to courses you have enrolled in. There are no expiration dates. You can review materials even after completing the course.

3. Self-Paced Learning

3.1 No Deadlines
Our courses are completely self-paced. There are no strict deadlines for completing modules or assignments. You set your own schedule.

3.2 Recommended Pacing
While there are no deadlines, we recommend dedicating 3-5 hours per week to each course to maintain momentum and retain information effectively.

3.3 Progress Tracking
Your Moodle dashboard shows your progress through each course, including completed modules, quiz scores, and assignment submissions.

4. Assessments and Grading

4.1 Quiz and Exam Policy
Most courses include quizzes to reinforce learning. You may retake quizzes as many times as needed. Your highest score will be recorded.

4.2 Assignments
Some courses require practical assignments, such as sermon outlines, business plans, or technical projects. Submit these through the Moodle assignment portal.

4.3 Passing Requirements
To receive a certificate, you must complete all modules and achieve an average score of 70% or higher on quizzes and assignments.

4.4 Grading Scale
90-100%: Excellent | 80-89%: Very Good | 70-79%: Satisfactory | Below 70%: Insufficient (retake required)

5. Academic Integrity

5.1 Original Work Policy
All assignments and quiz responses must be your own original work. Plagiarism, copying, or unauthorized collaboration is strictly prohibited.

5.2 Artificial Intelligence Use
AI tools may be used as learning aids but not to complete assignments on your behalf. If you use AI for research or drafting, you must disclose this in your submission.

5.3 Consequences of Violations
First violation: Warning and opportunity to resubmit. Second violation: Automatic failure of the assignment. Third violation: Account suspension and revocation of certificates.

6. Communication and Support

6.1 Technical Support
For login issues, platform errors, or technical problems, email support@livingoraclesofgod.com. Response within 48 hours.

6.2 Academic Support
For questions about course content, assignments, or grades, email academics@livingoraclesofgod.com.

6.3 WhatsApp Support
Reach us on WhatsApp at +233 24 145 6581 for urgent inquiries. Hours: Monday-Friday, 9am-5pm GMT.

6.4 Response Time
We strive to respond to all inquiries within 2 business days. During high-volume periods, response times may be longer.

7. Community Guidelines

7.1 Telegram Community
All enrolled students are invited to join our Telegram group for peer support, discussions, and prayer requests. The invitation link is sent via email after registration.

7.2 Respectful Communication
Treat all community members with respect. Harassment, hate speech, bullying, or discriminatory language will result in immediate removal from the group and possible account suspension.

7.3 Appropriate Content
Do not share inappropriate, offensive, or explicit content in any institute-affiliated channel. This includes spam, advertisements, or promotional messages.

7.4 Privacy in Groups
Respect the privacy of fellow students. Do not share personal information or screenshots from private conversations without permission.

8. Certificate Policy

8.1 Digital Certificates
Upon successfully completing a course, you can download your digital certificate directly from your Moodle dashboard. Digital certificates are free.

8.2 Printed Certificates
If you require a physical printed certificate, you may request one for a nominal fee to cover printing and shipping costs. Contact certificates@livingoraclesofgod.com for pricing and ordering.

8.3 Certificate Verification
Each certificate includes a unique verification ID. Employers, churches, or organizations can verify authenticity through our website's Verification Dashboard.

8.4 Lost Certificates
If you lose your certificate, you can re-download the digital version from Moodle at any time. For replacement printed certificates, contact certificates@livingoraclesofgod.com.

9. Technical Requirements

9.1 Internet Connection
A stable internet connection with at least 2 Mbps download speed is recommended. Our platform works on 3G/4G mobile networks.

9.2 Device Requirements
Our platform is accessible on smartphones, tablets, laptops, and desktop computers. Modern browsers such as Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge are recommended.

9.3 Moodle Mobile App
Download the Moodle app from Google Play Store or Apple App Store for offline access to course materials and mobile-friendly learning.

9.4 Required Software
Most courses require no additional software. Some technical courses may recommend free tools such as code editors or design software, which will be specified in the course materials.

10. Scholarship and Financial Aid

10.1 ADUSEI Foundation Scholarship
The ADUSEI Foundation provides full tuition scholarships to students from underserved communities, particularly across Africa. All students automatically qualify; no separate application is needed.

10.2 Data and Device Support
In special cases, the ADUSEI Foundation may provide internet data bundles or device assistance to students facing extreme financial hardship. Contact scholarships@livingoraclesofgod.com to inquire.

10.3 Voluntary Donations
Students who are able may choose to make voluntary donations to support the platform's operations and help fund scholarships for others. Donations are optional and non-refundable.

11. Grievance and Appeals

11.1 Grade Appeals
If you believe a quiz or assignment was graded incorrectly, you may submit an appeal within 14 days of receiving the grade. Email academics@livingoraclesofgod.com with your course name, assignment details, and explanation.

11.2 Policy Violation Appeals
If you believe an academic integrity violation or account suspension was issued in error, you may submit an appeal to appeals@livingoraclesofgod.com. Include your name, course, and supporting evidence.

11.3 Appeal Process
Appeals are reviewed by an academic committee within 14 business days. Decisions are final.

12. Withdrawal and Inactivity

12.1 Voluntary Withdrawal
If you wish to withdraw from a course, simply stop accessing it. There is no formal withdrawal process. You may resume at any time.

12.2 Extended Inactivity
Your account remains active indefinitely. There is no penalty for extended periods of inactivity. You can resume your studies whenever you are ready.

13. Code of Conduct Summary

As a student of The Living Oracles Global Institute, you agree to:

  • Complete your own work with honesty and integrity
  • Treat fellow students, staff, and instructors with respect
  • Use platform resources responsibly and ethically
  • Respect the intellectual property of the institute
  • Represent the institute positively in all interactions
